# Take methodology from external skill libraries, never authoring craft

- **Date:** 2026-06-02
- **Governs:** `plugins/*/skills/`

Public skill libraries overlap cc-foundry's domain. Their subject matter and methodology are ported into cc-foundry's
own structure, and their authoring conventions are not, at the cost of rewriting every port.

## Decision

- **Methodology, not craft.** An external skill library is a source of subject matter and methodology, never of
  authoring craft.
- **Ports take cc-foundry's shape.** Ported content takes cc-foundry's structure, description form, and format
  discipline; the library's conventions stay behind.
- **Surveys start from the craft check.** A survey of another library first checks its craft against these ADRs, then
  mines only methodology cc-foundry lacks.

## Why

- [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills), surveyed in full as the representative case, was weaker on
  every craft axis cc-foundry holds a position on: a "Use when [keywords]" description style, a fixed rule of about 100
  lines against [ADR 1.2](/design-docs/adr/1.2-skill-md-length.md), and no key-value-versus-table discipline
  ([ADR 1.1](/design-docs/adr/1.1-structured-data-shape.md)) (2026-06-02).
- Its edge was methodology in domains cc-foundry did not cover: Ousterhout's deep modules, DDD ubiquitous language,
  disciplined diagnosis.

## Consequences

- Every port is a rewrite, not a copy. Accepted.
- The surveyed methodology sits in `the-coder:coding`, `ai-helpers:skill-engineering`, `the-workflow:handoff`, and
  `the-blueprint:glossary` and `task-creation`; the dependency split became
  [ADR 1.3](/design-docs/adr/1.3-skill-dependencies.md).

## Rejected

- **The library's conventions along with its content** — weaker craft on every axis above.
- **Porting `write-a-skill`, `caveman`, `grill-me` / `grill-with-docs`, `to-prd` / `to-issues`, `zoom-out`** — thinner
  than cc-foundry's equivalents; not to be re-investigated.
- **`block-dangerous-git.sh`** — an unanchored-grep blocklist that ships false security.

## Revisit if

A library appears whose craft beats cc-foundry's on an axis these ADRs take a position on. Confidence not recorded.
